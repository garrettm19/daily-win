# Weekly learning loop

Daily evidence → deterministic `WeeklyLearningSummary` (`weekly_summary_v1`) → next-week scheduler context.

The LLM does not freely summarize raw notes as truth. If a friendly narrative is ever added, it must be generated **from** this summary and may not add facts.

## Storage

Prefer rebuildable derived summaries. If persisted later, record `week_start`, `week_end`, `summary_version`, source evidence range, and policy versions. Underlying events remain canonical.

## Weekly Coach Report (parent-facing)

- This week (wins completed, academic areas)
- What looked strong
- What still took work
- How they handled challenge
- What they enjoyed (explicit/repeated engagement only)
- What changes next week
- One thing for the parent (coaching suggestion, not a parent score)
- Optional Win of the Week (motivational memory, not new mastery)

Every factual claim is traceable to canonical evidence or deterministic derived state.

Acceptable: "Addition looked relatively automatic this week." "Writing still required more support."

Unacceptable: "Math kid." "Bad writer." "Lazy." "Low grit." "Natural athlete." "Visual learner." "Naturally gifted."

## Next-week preview

Primary/secondary academic focus, growth overlay, and why — from scheduler + learner state + review queue. No LLM reasoning.

## Engagement memory

Rebuildable. Affects theme/mechanic selection (including Bonus Challenges), **not** academic mastery. Enjoyment does not override what needs to be learned. Do not infer learning styles.

## EmergingNeedSignal

Repeated non-target observations may yield "consider diagnostic". They do not say the child is weak at that skill and do not change academic state until a real probe exists.

## School context (future-safe)

Parent-reported classroom notes may later influence diagnostic priority. They are PROFILE_REPORT, not Daily Win measurements. Do not upload school records or ingest report cards.

## Weekend vs weekday

Weekdays: open app → Your job today → 15–20 minutes → 3–5 reflection taps.

Weekend: Weekly Coach Report → what changed → what the system learned → next week's likely focus → optional Win of the Week.
