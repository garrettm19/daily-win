# Parent observation protocol

Mason should not constantly help. Behavior is more valuable than compliments.

Watch:

- where they pause
- where they reread
- where they tap the wrong control
- where they ask “what does this mean?”
- where they ignore text
- where they naturally start coaching
- whether they follow Your job today
- whether they reach for developer help
- whether they understand What to check
- whether reflection feels tedious
- whether Insight surprises them
- whether they notice adaptation the next day

Do not coach them through hidden mechanics unless something is broken.

Product ratings after the session are **app feedback**. They must never become child profile, learning events, academic state, growth state, or engagement memory.
