# Future product findings

Improvements noticed during V0.4.3 polish that need new product or data work. Not implemented.

## Win of the Week confirmation

**Finding:** Weekly Coach Report can list Win of the Week candidates, but the parent cannot persist a choice.

**Why it matters:** Confirmation would make the weekly ritual feel finished, like signing the paper summary.

**Recommended future version:** A later weekly-loop version. Needs a small durable field (chosen Win id + week). Do not bolt it onto polish.

## Newly available weekly badge

**Finding:** Home can link to Week, but there is no signal that a new report is ready.

**Why it matters:** Discoverability without interrupting today’s Win.

**Recommended future version:** When a cheap “report available this week” flag exists on Home state. Avoid an extra fetch on every Home focus.

## One-goal custom onboarding

**Finding:** Custom setup still requires 2–3 goals while the Hayes synthetic preset can demonstrate one academic goal plus growth.

**Why it matters:** Some families want a single academic focus without fake extra goals.

**Recommended future version:** Onboarding/schema change. Do not silently weaken the current 2–3 rule in polish.

## Parent-confirmed Win of the Week in learner memory

**Finding:** Selecting a Win of the Week could eventually inform engagement memory.

**Why it matters:** Stronger weekly loop — but it is new learner-state semantics.

**Recommended future version:** After weekly synthesis owns confirmed highlights. Not V0.4.x.
